Assumptions this tool makes
  • The limit is entered in GiB.
  • Period boundaries use your browser's local time, not the router's timezone.
  • Forecasting is done by averaging the daily usage over the period.
  • A router reboot is detected when the boot time (timestamp minus uptime) shifts by more than 2 minutes, or usage decreases between two rows.
